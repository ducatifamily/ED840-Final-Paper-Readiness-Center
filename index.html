Final component 4

Table of Contents Rescue Center

The included Applied Research Project Paper Template shows both a manual TOC approach and an autogenerated Word TOC approach. Select the method you are using and follow the matching checks.

Choose your TOC method above.
My headings are missing from the automatic TOC

Check whether the heading uses an actual Microsoft Word heading style. Manually bolding, enlarging, or indenting text does not make it a Word heading style.

My page numbers are wrong after I revised the paper

If you use an automatic TOC, update the entire table after revisions. If you use a manual TOC, recheck every page number after the paper is stable.

Something is appearing in the TOC that should not be there

For an automatic TOC, inspect the Word style applied to that text. A heading style may have been applied accidentally.

A long TOC entry wraps to a second line

Follow the current Applied Research Project Paper Template for the required presentation of long entries rather than improvising a new format.

Final paper mechanics

Formatting & readiness scan

Use these checks for document mechanics that can be addressed while content feedback is pending. Always defer to the current official template and course directions if requirements change.

Something looks wrong

Quick troubleshooting

Use these diagnostics before manually rebuilding the document.

My page numbers restarted, disappeared, or changed

Inspect section breaks before editing pagination. The template separates front matter from the main body so Roman and Arabic numbering can be handled differently. Deleting or changing a section break can disrupt numbering.

I have unexplained blank pages

Show formatting marks in Word and inspect page breaks and section breaks. Avoid deleting section breaks until you understand what they control.

My title is not identical everywhere

Compare the title page and abstract page carefully, including wording and line breaks when the template requires an exact match.

My abstract is over the allowed length

Return to the four-part blueprint: purpose, methodology, key findings, and final conclusions. Remove background detail that belongs elsewhere, but do not use this site to rewrite the abstract for you.

My heading formatting is inconsistent

Use Word styles consistently. Check that equivalent heading levels look the same throughout the paper and that chapter headings begin where the template requires.

My appendix or reference section is in the wrong place

Use the current Applied Research Project Paper Template and Chapter 1–5 outline to verify the required sequence. Do not rely on a prior term's paper as the formatting authority.
